# Notes — design and sourcing

## Why a broadsheet

The first edition of this day's page (an "extra edition" poster) was built on
the evening of 24 November, before the day had ended everywhere. This second
edition was researched across the full 24 November news cycle and rebuilt as
a dense newspaper front page, because the stories the evening edition missed
turned out to be among the day's most consequential:

- Ukraine's confirmed strikes on two Russian vessels off Libya — the war's
  reach beyond the Black Sea (Reuters' own lead item of the day).
- The second Houthi strike in a week on Riyadh's PIF Tower, 11 injured, and
  the coalition's answering strikes on Hodeidah (Reuters / Saudi civil
  defence).
- Russia's warning that Estonia's border closure risks "open war", and
  NATO's vow to defend space (Reuters / BBC).
- Shenzhou-24 actually launching on 24 November with Pakistan's first
  astronaut, Muhammad Zeeshan Ali (AP).
- Washington readying tariffs on China, the EU and others over subsidised
  production (AP sources, late on the 24th).

## Layout (1024×1024, all drawn in code)

- Ink top strip and double-ruled masthead; the date is named four times
  (strip, dateline, brief-grid header, caption logic) so the day is
  unmistakable.
- Lead: Chen Jining probe — headline, standfirst, four-sentence body, and a
  night skyline of Shanghai (Oriental Pearl tower, twisted tower, lit
  windows, harbour water) under a gold magnifying glass with a red star.
- Markets band in black with a rising gold sparkline and the day's index
  numbers as reported (S&P 500 +4.7%, Nasdaq +6.5%, Nikkei 225 +3.9%).
- Two second leads with drawn scenes: a warship under a flag with an
  explosion star (Libya), a lit tower with a red burst (Riyadh).
- Nine brief panels, each with its own drawn icon (rocket, ring of stars,
  tiered temple, island, dome, factory, coin, flag, shopfront), a bold
  headline and a two-line summary — twelve stories in all on the page,
  spanning China, Ukraine, Libya, Saudi Arabia, Gaza, Myanmar, the Pacific,
  Europe, South Asia, Latin America, trade and space.

## Faithfulness

Every number on the page is as reported that day: the Zaporizhzhia toll is
given as "kills 2 and wounds 40+" (AP's running count passed 40 wounded,
including children); the Riyadh toll is 11 injured (Saudi civil defence);
index moves are the day's reported closes. Where wires disagreed on a
detail, the page uses the formulation all of them support.
